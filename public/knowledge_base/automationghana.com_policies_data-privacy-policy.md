# Data Privacy Policy
#### INTRODUCTION
The Automaton Ghana Group is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our employees, clients, partners, suppliers, and all individuals who interact with us. This privacy policy applies to personal data you provide to The Automation Ghana Group through any of its three member companies (Process and Plant Automation Ltd, Automation Solutions Ltd, and Electrical Switchgear Ltd). Our policy details how we process personal data. This includes how we collect, use, disclose, store, discard and protect personal information shared with us via our website or any other platform, in accordance with the Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843).
Member companies of The Automation Ghana Group (TAGG) are the data controllers of the data we process unless otherwise stated, and shall be referred to as TAGG for the purpose of this policy.
#### INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We may collect and process the following types of personal data:
  1. **Personal Information** : Name, job title, company name, date of birth, I.D cards, photographs, TIN number, bank account details, Social Security Number, etc. (e.g., when you fill out a contact form or request information)
  2. **Contact Information:** Email address, phone number, postal address, etc.
  3. **Employment Information:** CVs, qualifications, and employment history are collected for recruitment and HR purposes.
  4. **Business Information:** Client and supplier records, contracts, invoices, and information provided when requesting our automation, engineering, compliance, sustainability, or consulting services.
  5. **Technical Data** : IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and usage patterns.
  6. **Compliance Data:** Information required for legal, regulatory, health, safety, or sustainability compliance.
  7. **Health Information:** Food vendors’ health data is collected to meet legal and legitimate business obligations. Employee health data is collected to assess fitness for work and to address possible future legal matters.


#### 1. HOW WE COLLECT PERSONAL DATA
The data we collect depends on the nature of your interaction with us. TAGG obtains personal information from you through the following channels:
  1. When you make an inquiry or request a quotation or any other information
  2. When you lodge a complaint to us about our product, process, or service
  3. When you register to attend any of our training or seminars (in-person or online)
  4. When you register to attend any of our events or conferences
  5. When your information is provided as part of the registration process as a supplier, vendor or contractor
  6. When you visit our website or any of our digital platforms
  7. When you respond to any of our surveys
  8. Throughout the product or service provision process, your data may be collected
  9. When you apply for a job
  10. When you are recruited as an employee (full or part-time), casual worker, or contract worker.
  11. When you serve as a guarantor for an employee.
  12. When you apply for or register with any of our Corporate Social Responsibility programs or products.
  13. When you are a beneficiary of our corporate philanthropy
  14. And through any other interaction with us


#### 2. PURPOSE OF DATA PROCESSING
We collect and process personal data for legitimate business purposes, including:
  1. Providing automation, engineering, compliance, and sustainability services
  2. Managing client and supplier relationships
  3. Recruitment, human resource management, and training
  4. Legal, regulatory, and contractual compliance
  5. Risk management, audits, and reporting
  6. Improving our services and operational efficiency
  7. Assessing fitness for work


#### 3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING
We ensure at all times that the personal data we collect is processed lawfully and reasonably, and that it does not infringe on your privacy rights protected under the Act. We guarantee that we will only process your data if the purpose is necessary, relevant, and not excessive. We will put in place measures to ensure you’re informed about the purpose of processing your data. We will only use the data you provide for the specific purpose for which it was collected.
We process personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:
  1. Consent of the data subject
  2. Performance of a contract
  3. Compliance with legal obligations
  4. Legitimate business interests, provided these do not override individual rights
  5. Vital interest
  6. Public interest


**Where we rely on your consent to process your personal data, you can withdraw your consent at any time**. In the event that you withdraw your consent, we may not be able to provide certain services to you. We will, however, advise you if this is the case when you withdraw your consent.
#### 4. DATA SHARING AND DISCLOSURE
We shall only disclose information you provide to us after we have sought and obtained your consent to the disclosure of the same, or where the initial disclosure was made for the purposes of it being made available to the public under the provisions of Act 843.
We shall not disclose information you provide us which is not at the time of the disclosure, or has not previously been available to the public from other sources.
We shall not make your information available to a third party unless this is necessary for the purpose for which you provided the information or unless we are required to do so under Act 843 or other relevant laws in Ghana, or international treaties/agreements or conventions.
The data we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the choices you make, including your privacy settings, the purpose of your visit to our website, and the products and features you use.
We may share your personal data with your consent or to complete a transaction or provide a service for you.
We do not sell personal data. We may share personal data with:
  1. Employees and authorised representatives of TAGG
  2. Regulators and government authorities (to fulfil a legal obligation)
  3. Professional advisers (legal, audit, insurance)
  4. Third-party service providers acting under strict confidentiality and data protection obligations


#### 5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel only. TAGG implements **appropriate technical and organisational measures** to safeguard personal data against:
  1. Unauthorised access
  2. Loss, misuse, or alteration
  3. Accidental disclosure


#### 6. DATA RETENTION
Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to:
  1. Fulfil the purpose for which it was collected
  2. Meet legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations


We shall not retain your data for a period longer than is necessary to achieve the purpose for which we process your data, and in line with our **retention policy**. Specific details of the retention periods for different aspects of your personal data are available in our retention policy, which you can request from us.
At the end of the retention period, your data will either be deleted completely or anonymised, so that it can be used in a non-identifiable way for statistical analysis, business planning or to fulfil other compliance obligations. In such instances, we may use this information indefinitely without further notice to you.
#### 7. YOUR RIGHTS
As a data subject, you have rights under Act 843, and you can exercise any of these rights by serving a written notice that clearly states the reasons why you need to access the data. We will request specific information from you to verify your identity and confirm your right to access your personal data (or to exercise any of your other rights). If we need further information before responding to your request, we will contact you to ask for additional details.
Your request will be responded to within 21 days and processed in accordance with **our Data Subject Access Request Procedure**. In our reply, we will inform you whether we have complied with, intend to comply with, or do not intend to comply with your request. If we do not intend to comply, we will provide the reasons for our decision. We will also notify you of the action taken in response to the request.
The following are some of your rights; 
**a.****The right to be informed**
You have the right to be informed about the purpose of data collection and how your data will be processed
**b.****Right to give and withdraw consent**
Although you can withdraw your consent, this does not invalidate any processing that has already taken place. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before the consent was withdrawn
**c.****Right to prevent automated decision-making**
When decision-making is automated, you have the right to object or prevent the processing of your data. You will be informed of the consequences of opting out of this process.
**d.****The right to object to data processing**
You have the right to object to our processing of your data. If we rely on your legitimate interest or that of a third party, and there is something about your particular situation that makes you wish to object to the processing, you can do so on the grounds that you believe it affects your fundamental rights and freedoms.
**e.****Right to amend**
You have the right to request that we correct your data if it is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading, obtained unlawfully, or incompatible with the legitimate purposes for which the data was collected
**f.** **Right to prevent processing of personal data**
**g. Right to complain**
At any point in time, as a data subject, you can complain to TAGG about the processing of your data.
**h.** **Right to request erasure**
You can also request that we destroy or delete a record of personal data about you that we no longer have your consent to retain.
**i.****Request for data**
You can request that we provide a description of the data we hold about you, and the identity of any third party or category of third parties who have or have had access to that data.
**j. Right to prevent processing for direct marketing**
#### 8. OUR RESPONSIBILITIES
We have a duty to protect your privacy and any personal data we collect from you through your interactions with us. Before processing any data, we will ensure that the data we receive from you is complete, accurate, up-to-date, and not misleading, taking into account the purpose for which we collect or process the data.
We shall also indicate which data you have discretion to provide, and or which data we think you have to provide because it’s mandatory to do so. We shall notify you of the consequences if you fail to provide the said data.
#### 9. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
Where personal data is transferred outside Ghana, TAGG ensures appropriate safeguards are in place to protect data in line with applicable data protection laws.
#### 10. COOKIES AND WEBSITE USE
Cookies are tiny files that are downloaded to your computer to improve your experience. TAGG uses cookies to ensure our website functions properly and to help us understand how visitors interact with our site, enhancing your future visits. You can set your browser to notify you when “cookies” are being transferred or to reject “cookies”, but this may prevent you from using some of our web pages. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.
#### 11. EXTERNAL LINKS
Our website may include links to third-party websites. TAGG is not responsible for the privacy policies, practicies or content of external sites.
#### 12. OUR COMMITMENT TO YOU
We are committed to managing & safeguarding your personal information.
We are guided not only by Act 843 but also by the General Principles of Data Protection and international best practices in Data Protection. At all times, our aim is to be responsible and ensure the integrity of your data.
We assure you that whenever you provide personal information, we will handle it in accordance with this Privacy Policy and always strive to keep your data accurate and secure.
#### 13. UPDATES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, regulations, or our operations. Any updates will be communicated through our official channels, as applicable.
